# Scoring and decisions

## 1-4 scale

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | Strong hire: clear, specific evidence above the bar |
| 3 | Hire: meets the bar with specific evidence |
| 2 | Concern: thin, generic or mixed evidence |
| 1 | No hire: clear evidence against, or a deal breaker observed |

No half points; no score without a quote or observed behavior.

## Writing behavioral anchors

For every scored competency, describe what 1, 2, 3 and 4 look like in observable behavior, tied to the probes used. Example anchors:

| Competency | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Courage | Agreed with a confident wrong claim; caved in role-play; doing right never cost them | Disagreed only with heavy hedging; conceded under authority | Challenged traps with reasons; held firm while staying curious; names a real cost | Held the standard and owned the other side's problem; would pay the cost again; knows when holding firm was wrong |
| Grit | Gave up when stuck; no story with a real low point | Persisted only with heavy hints; vague low point | Kept reasoning through the recovery ladder; specific low point and what they changed | Turned being stuck into better questions; knows when to quit vs. push |
| AI-native, self-optimizing | No recent use, or accepted AI output wholesale | Generic use; never improved own workflow unasked | Specific, recent, everyday use; knows failure modes; caught the planted flaw | Built tools or workflows others adopted; verification is second nature |
| Transparency | Hid or delayed own error; bluffed | Disclosed only when asked; vague timeline | Same-day disclosure with names; says "I don't know" plainly | Disclosed to people who'd never have found it; changed a team habit |
| Core reasoning (hard to teach) | Can't reason about the fundamentals even with the ladder | Textbook recall; stalls without hints | Correct reasoning; hypotheses before fixes | Diagnoses fluidly; transfers across domains |
| Domain knowledge (trainable) | Never a sole reason to reject | Little context; learns fast from a primer | Working knowledge | Deep experience |
| Recovery when stuck | Bluffs, freezes or gets defensive | Guesses without reasoning | Reasons from first principles or transfers | Turns the gap into sharp questions |

## Decision rules (set before interviewing)

- **Veto:** an evidence-backed 1 on any value ends the process; any interviewer can invoke it.
- **Gates:** e.g. after culture screen, any value 1 or two values 2 → stop; after skill screen, core reasoning 2 or lower, or bluffing, or agreeing with both traps → stop. Domain knowledge alone never stops a candidate.
- **Hire bar:** no 1s; hiring priorities and core reasoning each 3+; values average 3.0+.
- **Domain-gap hire:** a 2 on trainable knowledge only with a named mentor and written 90-day plan agreed at the debrief.
- **Split or thin evidence:** a 30-minute follow-up within 2 days, never a hire on a guess. Weight a said-vs-done gap more than one low score.

## Scorecard template

```
Candidate / Background (domain or adjacent) / Round / Interviewer / Date:

| Competency | Score (1-4) | One piece of evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |

Trap or planted error caught? (yes / no + what they said)
"I don't know" or bluffing moments:
Domain gaps a mentor would cover:
Candidate questions: count and best example
Deal breaker observed? (yes / no + evidence)
Partner interviewer only - would I seek this person out? (yes / no + reason)
Recommendation: Strong hire / Hire / No hire / Strong no hire
```

Recruiting pre-fills candidate, round and assigned competencies so it takes about 5 minutes.

## Debrief (10 minutes)

Everyone reads the summary sheet before joining; no one presents notes.

| Time | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Veto check: any flagged 1? Decide on that evidence only |
| 2-6 | Flagged disagreements (2+ apart) and thin values only; compare said vs. done |
| 6-8 | Recommendations, most junior first |
| 8-10 | HM decides and records the reason; name the mentor for any domain gap |

## Calibration loop

- Pilot every technical problem on a current team member.
- Track which questions draw "I don't know"; after 3-4 candidates, add context or replace miscalibrated ones. Don't lower the values bar.
- At 90 days and 12 months, compare each hire's scores with performance and retention; keep predictive probes, drop the rest.
- Track days from first contact to offer and where candidates waited; fix the slowest step first.

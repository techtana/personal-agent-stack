# Checklist for a new role

**Define**
- [ ] 12-month outcomes for the role.
- [ ] Two to four hiring priorities and the team values, each as observable behavior.
- [ ] Deal breakers.
- [ ] Requirements split into hard-to-teach and trainable; mentor and ramp plan for trainable gaps.
- [ ] Adjacent talent pools and the gap each brings.
- [ ] What the role honestly offers and its hard parts (motivation match, realistic preview).

**Design**
- [ ] Coverage table: every trait with a stated and an observed probe.
- [ ] Probes for motivation match (retention) and learning velocity and coachability (potential).
- [ ] Rounds ordered by cost and disqualification rate, each with a stop rule.
- [ ] Per-candidate time budget; each round scripted with a timing table and one skip-if-short block.
- [ ] Standard calendar lengths; scorecard time blocked after each call.
- [ ] 1-4 anchors for every scored competency, and the hire bar.
- [ ] Traps, role-plays, planted errors and review tasks prepared; technical problems piloted on the team.

**Set up for speed**
- [ ] Target time from first contact to offer, and maximum gap between steps.
- [ ] Standing interviewer holds for each round.
- [ ] Offer approvals agreed before the final round.
- [ ] Recruiting handed the keyword guide, pre-screen script, prep email, scorecard templates and reference script.
- [ ] Every interviewer briefed on rules, fairness and their assigned signals.

**Run and learn**
- [ ] Same-day go/no-go after each gate; debrief within 24 hours of the final round.
- [ ] A note to every candidate after every round with the next step.
- [ ] Follow-up on thin evidence within 2 days instead of guessing.
- [ ] After 3-4 candidates, check question health and adjust.
- [ ] At 90 days and 12 months, compare scores with performance and retention; update the probes.
